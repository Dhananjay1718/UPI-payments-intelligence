Which matters more when prioritizing payment issues: the highest failure rate or the largest number of affected transactions?

That question shaped my UPI Payments Intelligence project, built with Python, SQL and Power BI.

I worked through data cleaning, SQL analysis and a five-page Power BI report covering national trends, merchant activity, bank reliability and geographic coverage.

Three findings from the August 2026 snapshot:

• The top five of 29 listed merchant categories contributed 67.10% of their combined transaction volume.
• Among six selected sending banks, Baroda U.P. Bank had the highest technical-decline rate, while Central Bank of India had the largest estimated number of declines: 11.19 million.
• 44.39% of reported state-table volume was geographically unclassified, a limitation that matters before recommending expansion.

I also modeled a hypothetical 0.50% decline-rate target with 50% gap closure. It implied 6.74 million fewer declines in the selected sample—an assumption-based scenario, not an achieved saving.

My main learning: useful analysis depends on clear denominators, weighted metrics and knowing what the data cannot prove.
